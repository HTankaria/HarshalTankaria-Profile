## LaTeX / Math Equations

Inline math: $Z = R + jX$ or $S_{11} = \frac{Z_L - Z_0}{Z_L + Z_0}$

Block equation (display mode):

$$
\Gamma = \frac{Z_L - Z_0}{Z_L + Z_0}
$$

$$
\mathbf{S} =
\begin{bmatrix}
  S_{11} & S_{12} \\
  S_{21} & S_{22}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Multi-line aligned equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
  P_{reflected} &= |\Gamma|^2 \cdot P_{incident} \\
  \text{VSWR}   &= \frac{1 + |\Gamma|}{1 - |\Gamma|}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Smith Charts & RF Plots

Export your Smith chart from your tool of choice as a **PNG or SVG** and drop it
in the `assets/` folder next to this file.

**From common tools:**
- **MATLAB** → `saveas(fig, 'smith.svg')` or `exportgraphics`
- **Python (scikit-rf)** → `network.plot_s_smith(); plt.savefig('smith.svg')`
- **ADS / Microwave Office** → File → Export → SVG/PNG
- **Qucs / LTspice** → screenshot or export

Then embed it like any image:

![Smith chart](./assets/smith-chart.svg)
